SOLARIS | NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Challenge: Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer  

Team Hyperion

About Our Project

SOLARIS is our team's submission for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, developed in response to the challenge "Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer."

Our project aims to introduce young learners to the challenges of space exploration through an interactive mission-training experience.
